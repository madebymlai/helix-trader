# Databento definitions

`fill-catalog` fills the definitions of `GLBX` instruments from Databento's `GLBX.MDP3` dataset, and of `OPRA` instruments, US option contracts, from its `OPRA.PILLAR`, through the [node fill](../../catalog/README.md), each request naming Databento's client, Nautilus' data engine writing each back to the catalog; their history is [LSE](../lse/README.md)'s, and [IB](../ib/README.md) serves their live data and trades them (see the [routing table](../mod.rs)). A contract's definition is asked in LSE's fill of its series, before them, over the span of what the catalog lacks of them; once the catalog holds it, Nautilus answers later requests from it and Databento is not asked. Set `DATABENTO_API_KEY` in the environment or `.env`; it is read, and the dataset's end checked, whenever a `GLBX` or `OPRA` series lacks something.

Before starting the node, the fill checks the available end of each dataset it asks, the one Nautilus' client asks a venue's instruments of (its publishers' map: `GLBX.MDP3` for `GLBX`, `OPRA.PILLAR` for `OPRA`). A dataset lags real time, so an end beyond its available range is refused.

Dated contracts are stored under their own IDs, in Nautilus' two-digit year (`GCZ26.GLBX`), Databento's default and LSE's; rolled roots fill their dated contracts over their respective slices, not the root itself. An option contract is stored under its OSI symbol, padded, under `OPRA` (`AAPL  261120C00355000.OPRA`), the venue of the `OPRA.PILLAR` publisher that sends its definitions. Backtest and live IDs must be chosen deliberately: Databento's default venue differs from IB's; see [instrument identity](../../catalog/README.md).

Offline tests cover the dataset's end and the requests Databento takes in `src/venues/databento`; `GLBX` and `OPRA` fills against mock Databento and LSE peers, Databento's peer replaying its captured definitions of GCZ6 and of AAPL's 355 call of 20 November 2026 (`testdata/`, see `testing.rs`), are in `src/venues/lse`.
