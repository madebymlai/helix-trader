Commission
Your commission is your percentage of the amount the customer pays us for the referred subscription, after any discount and excluding tax.

Where a customer pays in pounds sterling or euros, we convert that payment to US dollars at our exchange rate when the commission is recorded, and your commission is your percentage of the converted amount.
